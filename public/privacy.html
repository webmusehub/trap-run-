Privacy Policy — Trap Run

Last updated: October 2026

Trap Run ("we", "our", or "us"), provided by WebMuseHub, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you play Trap Run and how that information is used.

1. Information We Collect

Trap Run is designed to collect the minimum necessary data to provide game functionality and global leaderboards:

  • Anonymous Player Identifier: A randomly generated unique identifier stored locally on your device to associate your leaderboard scores. We do not require passwords, email addresses, or personal accounts.
  • Display Name: The custom runner name you choose for global leaderboard display.
  • Gameplay Statistics: Level completion times, coin counts, death counts, and unlocked levels.
  • Lightweight Analytics: Non-personally identifiable game events (such as game start, level start, level complete, victory) to help us analyze level balance and gameplay difficulty.
  • Local Storage: We use browser localStorage to persist your game progress, audio settings, display name, and player ID locally on your device.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected data strictly to:

  • Save and restore your game progression and settings locally.
  • Display rankings on the global and per-level leaderboards.
  • Improve gameplay design, fix bugs, and balance level difficulty.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Anonymous leaderboard scores are publicly displayed on the global leaderboard screen. Third-party infrastructure providers (such as Supabase for database hosting) process leaderboard requests securely.

4. Advertising & Analytics

If advertising features are enabled in future updates, non-personalized ad delivery containers may load network scripts. No personally identifiable user information is shared with advertisers.

5. Data Control & Deletion

You can reset your local save data and profile at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this domain.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact WebMuseHub support.
