FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about Trap Run.

What is Trap Run?
Trap Run is a fast-paced 2D browser platformer where levels are filled with hidden traps, fake exits, moving platforms, and coins. Your goal is to navigate all 10 handcrafted levels in record time with as few deaths as possible.
Is Trap Run free?
Yes, Trap Run is 100% free to play directly in your web browser with no downloads or installs required.
Can I play on mobile?
Absolutely! Trap Run features built-in responsive touch controls for mobile smartphones and tablets. It plays best in landscape mode.
How many levels are there?
There are 10 handcrafted levels featuring unique trap mechanics, 100 total collectible coins, and progressive speedrun difficulty.
How do I get on the leaderboard?
Simply enter a runner display name when starting the game, complete any level, and your verified speedrun completion time will automatically submit to the live global leaderboard!
